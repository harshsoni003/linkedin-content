# Tracker

Four statuses. Nothing skips a step.

| Status | Means | Requirement to enter |
|---|---|---|
| Drafted | Written, not checked | Has a full body |
| In review | Being checked now | Nothing cleared yet |
| Ready | Cleared to publish | Passed a full editing read |
| Dated | Has a publish date | Has a slot on the calendar |

- Everything pooling in **Drafted** → writing is happening, checking is not
- Everything in **Ready**, nothing **Dated** → posts finished, not going out
