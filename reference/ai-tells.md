# AI Tells

Readers scroll past machine writing all day. They've learned the pattern.

When your post carries these, they don't think *"AI helped with this."* They think *"this person has nothing to say"* — and that sticks to your name, not to the tool.

Check every draft against this list.

## The tells

| Tell | Example | Why it reads as machine |
|---|---|---|
| **"It's not X, it's Y"** | "It's not an expense, it's an investment." | The single most recognizable AI construction |
| **Parallel repetition** | "More leads. More calls. More revenue." | Humans escalate. Models echo. |
| **Triads everywhere** | A three-item list in every paragraph | A model habit, not a human one |
| **Rhetorical transitions** | "So what does this actually mean?" | Filler dressed as engagement. A person just says the thing. |
| **Vendor grammar** | seamless, leverage, unlock, game-changer, elevate, delve, powered by | Nobody talks like this about their own work |
| **Filler transitions** | "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing", "At the end of the day", "In today's fast-paced world" | Adds nothing |
| **Pick-two-audiences** | "Whether you're a founder or a freelancer..." | Written for everyone, lands with no one |
| **"Imagine..." openers** | "Imagine waking up to..." | Generic by design. A real moment beats an imagined one. |
| **Fake hedges** | "roughly $4,000" when you know it was $4,310 | Drains the claim |
| **Celebrating an absence** | "0 drafts lost" | A non-event to feel nothing about |
| **Uniform sentence length** | Every line the same length | Humans vary. Models don't. |
| **Conclusion restates opening** | | Reads like a book report |

## How to fix repetition

**Escalate.** Each line must add information, raise the stakes, or move the argument.

Keep the strongest line. Rewrite the echoes.

## What NOT to flag

**Your own real phrasing.** If you genuinely talk in qualifiers, that's your voice, not a tell. Your actual writing outranks this table.

The point is removing AI-invented habits, not sanding you down until you sound like nobody.

## Also check

- **Em dashes** — only if your own writing uses them. Otherwise use a period or comma.
- **No number you didn't supply.** Ever.
- **No emoji** unless your own writing uses them.
- **Exclamation points near zero.** Urgency comes from the proof, not the punctuation.
- **No dense 5-sentence blocks.** Line breaks are pacing.

## The contradiction check

Read line 1 against line 2. Then line 2 against the body. Then the ending against the opening.

Find any place a later line undoes an earlier claim.

This is the failure that kills otherwise strong posts — and you are the least likely person to spot it in your own writing.

## The final test

Read the clean version next to the original.

Every change should sound **more like you**, not just less like AI. If a rewrite sounds like a second robot with a different accent, the editor didn't have enough of your voice to work with. Give it your voice profile and run it again.
