# Post Format

## The shape

```
Line 1     HOOK — opens a question the reader must keep reading to close
Line 2     Adds NEW information. Never repeats line 1.

------------------ see more ------------------

Line 3     Under 10 words. Re-promise the payoff.
           "Here's what that actually cost."

Body       Conclusion early — the best thought doesn't wait for paragraph 4
           Scene before principle — let them picture it, then give the rule
           Concrete beats abstract — "a $340 invoice", not "a small charge"
           Vary sentence length — long explaining line, then a short landing line

Ending     Depends on your goal (below)
```

## Two hard rules for the opening

1. **Don't answer the question before the cut.** If the reader can guess the ending from the visible lines, the post is dead.
2. **Don't open and close the loop in one breath.**
   > ❌ "Most bookkeepers miss deadlines because they're disorganized. Get organized."

## Line 2 — where most posts die

It must **carry line 1's last beat forward**. If line 1 ends on a number, line 2 stays with that number. Change the subject and the reader leaves.

It must **add information**. If deleting it changes nothing, it's repetition in a costume.

It must **not contradict** line 1.

Two shapes that work:
- **Resolve the objection** — answer the doubt line 1 just created
- **Before-state scene** — drop them into the mess the claim came out of

## Pick ONE goal

A post chasing two goals achieves neither.

| Goal | How it ends |
|---|---|
| **Gain followers** | On the insight worth following for. No ask. |
| **Trust with buyers** | Show how you think or work. No pitch — the proof sells. |
| **Collect leads** | Point at the thing once, plainly. Give the exact word to comment. |
| **Start conversations** | One direct question they can answer in one line. |

Never end on a motivational poster. Never end on "Agree?"

## Length

- Most posts: **120–250 words**
- A story can earn **300**
- A lead post stays **short** — the giveaway is the payoff, not the post

Guidance, not law. But "it feels right" is not a reason.

## Numbers

Winning posts carry real numbers on far more lines than losing posts.

But forcing a digit onto every line reads like a spreadsheet.

**Only ever use numbers you actually have.** Missing ones stay marked:
```
[YOUR NUMBER: what the software costs per month]
```

A blank is safe. An invented number that gets challenged in the comments is not.
