# How @AdityaShips writes on X

Built from his export: **330 posts + 391 replies, 18 Feb → 21 Sep 2026** (7 months).
Every number below comes from that data. The posts themselves are in [top-posts.md](top-posts.md).

**Use this for the style. Never for the facts.** His numbers, his mom, his exams are his. When writing for me, use my real facts or mark the gap `[NUMBER: what goes here]`.

## Who he is (so the style makes sense)

Indian student in Mumbai, 4th year of uni, building small paid apps in public: Appthetics (AI mobile app design), SleekDemo (Mac screen recorder), Sleek Analytics, NotchOwl (Mac notch app). His whole account is one story: **a kid making his first internet money, told one sale at a time.**

The reader is other indie hackers who want the same thing. Every post answers "is it working?"

## What flops (avoid)

| What | Example | ❤️ |
|---|---|---|
| "Btw I built this with my tool" plugs | "Btw i made this using Appthetics, It's a tool that lets you…" | 7 |
| Asking for feedback | "would love to know your feedback / Try here -" | 5 |
| Long explanations | "Some folks got confused. It took me around 2 hours to go from idea…" | 4 |
| "Coming soon" with nothing to show | "Been working non stop on the brand new UI… $10k mrr loading" | 0 |
| Anything over 280 characters | 28 posts, median 38 | — |

Plugs and explanations belong in replies under a post that's already doing well, not in new posts.

## Voice rules

**Sound**
- Talks like a group chat, not a blog. `bro`, `lol`, `lmao`, `wtf`, `rn`, `atp`, `btw`, `fr`, `holy shit`, `cooked`, `banger`, `lock in`
- `u` / `ur` instead of you / your, sometimes
- Newer posts are often all lowercase. Both work; lowercase reads more casual
- Swears lightly (`fck`, `shi`) and says when he's shaking or crying. Real reactions, not polished ones

**Shape**
- One idea per post. Most posts are 1–3 lines
- A blank line between each sentence or beat
- Lists use `>` or `-`, never numbered essays
- No hashtags. Ever
- Links go in the post only for launches

**Numbers**
- Always exact: `$376 MRR`, `20 days`, `19 sales in a single day`, `$21 in the last 24 hours`
- Small numbers are fine. `$7` and `0 paid users today` build trust
- A time frame makes a number land: "in 75 days", "9 hour old product"

**Emoji**
- 0–2 per post, usually at the end of a line
- His set: 😭 (overwhelmed/happy) · 🫶 (grateful) · 🎉 (launch) · 👀 (tease) · 😂 · 💪
- Never emoji bullets in normal posts (only in the income report)

**Feeling**
- Gratitude is part of the voice: "Thanku X 🫶", "always grateful", "God is great"
- Humble brag, not a lecture. He never tells you what to do, he shows what happened

**Never**
- Corporate words: "excited to announce", "leverage", "game-changer", "thrilled"
- Threads with 1/ 2/ 3/. His thread-style replies got 10 likes
- Explaining the joke or the screenshot

## When he posts

Most posts go out 18:00–23:00 IST. His medians are a bit higher 11:00–14:00 IST (~80 vs ~60), but the sample is small, so treat it as a hint, not a rule.

His median fell from **101 in March to 36 in September** as more posts became quick reactions and quote tweets. His early months of milestones + family + daily logs were the strongest run.

## Checklist before a post goes out

1. Is it one idea? Cut the second one.
2. Is there an exact number or a real moment in line 1?
3. Under 280 characters? If not, cut.
4. Does it sound like a text to a friend? Read it out loud.
5. Screenshot or image attached if there's anything to prove.
6. No hashtags, no "excited to announce", no "btw try my tool".
7. Every number is mine and true. Missing ones stay `[NUMBER: …]`.
