# The 11 Skills

What each one does, what to feed it, what comes back.

Full instructions live in the skill files themselves. This is the map.

---

## Start with these 3

### voice-match
**Do this once.** Everything else depends on it.

- **Give it:** your name, role, audience + 3-10 posts you wrote. No posts? Say "interview me" and answer 6 questions.
- **Get back:** a voice profile under 25 lines — your openings, rhythm, words you use, words you'd never use, and 3 lines that sound exactly like you.
- **Then:** save it in `../voice-profile.md` and paste it into every session.

Test it: read each line and ask *could this describe half of LinkedIn?* A good profile fails that on every line.

### post-writer
- **Give it:** your voice profile + 1-3 sentences about something that actually happened + your goal.
- **Get back:** one draft, three alternate openings, and a recommendation.
- **Key rule:** it never invents numbers. Missing facts come back marked `[YOUR NUMBER: what goes here]`.

A moment beats a topic:
> ❌ "something about cash flow"
> ✅ "Client thought March was profitable. The books showed why the cash was gone."

### voice-firewall
**Run on every draft before it goes out.**

- **Give it:** the full draft + your voice profile.
- **Get back:** a score /10, every robot line rewritten, PASS or FAIL, and the clean version.

See `../reference/ai-tells.md` for what it hunts.

## Add later

### angle-finder
Dump your messy wins, numbers, opinions, failures → get **30 post angles**, each 6 words or less, each traced back to the item it came from.

> `Compliance is invisible until expensive`
> `Cash in the bank isn't profit`
> `Growth hides margin rot`

Fixes: "I don't know what to post."

### post-brief
Locks the decisions **before** you draft: goal, hook family, 5 hooks with a pick, line two, the one proof point, target length, and every shaky claim.

Fixes: rewriting the same draft four times.

### hook-writer
One idea → **10 openings**, ranked, spread across at least 5 families, winner named.

Fixes: nobody stopped scrolling.

### visual-brief
Finished post → exact image instructions. What it must *prove*, the layout, every word on the image, the size and crop.

Not "modern and clean." The actual structure.

### calendar
Finished drafts + a start date → a dated table with a reason on every row. Never puts two "comment for this" posts back to back.

Fixes: 3 posts in one week, then a month of silence.

### tracker
One table. Four statuses: **Drafted → In review → Ready → Dated**.

It refuses to mark something Ready if you skipped the check. See `../tracker.md`.

### scoreboard
20+ past posts with their numbers → your **median**, every post tiered, worst first.

- **Hit** = 5x your median or better
- **Solid** = at or above
- **Flat** = quarter-median to median
- **Dead** = under a quarter

Median, never average — one viral post wrecks an average.

Worst first on purpose: you already reread your best post ten times.

### pattern-audit
Your scoreboard → which *opening type* produced your winners.

Ranks on **hit rate**, not median. A family can have an average median and still be twice as likely to produce a Hit. That's the one to bet on.

Ends with one recommendation, not seven.

---

## The order

```
Once:     voice-match

Monthly:  angle-finder → post-brief → post-writer
                      → voice-firewall → visual-brief
                      → calendar → tracker

Month end: scoreboard → pattern-audit → back to angle-finder
```

Month-end results feed next month's plan. It gets sharper each round.
