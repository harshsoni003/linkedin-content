# Example: angle-finder

Dana Reyes runs Ledgerline, a six-person bookkeeping firm serving owner-led small businesses. She has run it for eleven years. Dana, the firm, the clients, and every number in this example are fictional.

## What Dana pasted

> - Caught $48,000 in unfiled sales tax in a new client's first week
> - I think most e-commerce founders read their P&L wrong. They manage cash and call it profit.
> - Fired our biggest client last year, 30% of revenue. Best call I made.
> - Every client email answered in under 4 hours. We track it.
> - Lost $12,000 on a hire I rushed.
> - Went from side project to $720,000 a year in 3 years.
> - Moved all 34 clients to flat monthly pricing in January. Revenue per client up 22%, one client left.

## What angle-finder returned

**1. The material, numbered**

1. The $48,000 unfiled sales tax catch
2. Founders misreading their P&L (opinion)
3. Fired the biggest client, 30% of revenue
4. Every email answered under 4 hours, tracked
5. $12,000 lost on a rushed hire
6. Side project to $720,000 a year in 3 years
7. All 34 clients moved to flat pricing, revenue per client up 22%

**2. The angle inside each item**

1. Clean books still hide tax bombs
2. Founders manage cash, call it profit
3. Your biggest client can shrink you
4. Speed is a trust system
5. Rushed hiring bills you later
6. Boring services scale quietly
7. Flat pricing filters bad clients

Items 3, 5, and 7 form one recurring theme: expensive decisions that paid off. Items 1 and 4 form a second, thinner pair: operations you can verify.

**4. The reruns, in one place**

Angles 5, 13, 17, and 28. All four are worth rerunning; the firing story and the $48,000 catch are the two strongest assets here and can each come back every few weeks wearing a new opening. Just post them as returns to a story, not as news.

**5. The needs-proof-first list**

- "We save clients money on average." Needs a measured average across the 34 clients, not two good stories. Unlock: pull the number, then claim it.
- "We've never missed a filing deadline." "Never" needs an internal check across every client and every year before it ships. Unlock: run the check; if it holds, this is a monster claim.
- "E-commerce brands overpay for bookkeeping." As a market claim it needs real pricing comparisons. Honest today only as an opinion, framed as one.
