# Example: calendar

Dana Reyes runs Ledgerline, a six-person bookkeeping firm serving owner-led small businesses. She has run it for eleven years. Dana, the firm, the clients, and every number in this example are fictional.

## What Dana pasted

> Use calendar on these 11 drafts. Start September 1. Nothing on weekends.
> 1. "We fired our biggest client in March." UNGATED
> 2. "The Month-End Close Checklist" GATED, comment word CLOSE
> 3. "Profit First nearly broke two of my clients." UNGATED
> 4. "The Sales Tax Exposure Map" GATED, comment word MAP
> 5. "Your ad spend is hiding in six line items." UNGATED
> 6. "A client asked me to hide $40,000 from their books." UNGATED
> 7. "The Cash Flow Forecast template" GATED, comment word FORECAST
> 8. "I spent 11 years pricing bookkeeping wrong." UNGATED
> 9. "Accrual accounting is overkill for most stores under $5M." UNGATED
> 10. "34 e-commerce brands. The same three bookkeeping mistakes." UNGATED
> 11. "The 1099 deadline nobody puts in their calendar." UNGATED

## What calendar returned

**1. Batch summary**

11 drafts: 3 gated, 8 ungated. Healthy balance, nothing to flag.

**2. Planning choice**

Dana did not give a weekly frequency, so the 11 ready posts are spread across the full 30 days. That spacing is a planning default, not a prediction about performance. The three gated posts are separated by ungated posts, and the subjects vary across the month.

**3. The dated table**

| Date | Day | Post | Gated | Why this slot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sep 1 | Tue | "We fired our biggest client in March." | No | Open with a first-person client decision |
| Sep 3 | Thu | "The Month-End Close Checklist" (CLOSE) | Yes | First gated post, after an ungated story |
| Sep 7 | Mon | "Profit First nearly broke two of my clients." | No | Return to an ungated corrective story |
| Sep 9 | Wed | "34 e-commerce brands. The same three bookkeeping mistakes." | No | Change the opening shape from story to number |
| Sep 11 | Fri | "A client asked me to hide $40,000 from their books." | No | Put a client story between the two gated posts |
| Sep 15 | Tue | "The Sales Tax Exposure Map" (MAP) | Yes | Second gated post, separated by three ungated posts |
| Sep 17 | Thu | "Your ad spend is hiding in six line items." | No | Move from tax exposure to expense review |
| Sep 21 | Mon | "I spent 11 years pricing bookkeeping wrong." | No | Change back to a first-person business decision |
| Sep 23 | Wed | "Accrual accounting is overkill for most stores under $5M." | No | Follow the pricing story with a defensible opinion |
| Sep 28 | Mon | "The Cash Flow Forecast template" (FORECAST) | Yes | Final gated post, after three ungated posts |
| Sep 30 | Wed | "The 1099 deadline nobody puts in their calendar." | No | End the month with an ungated deadline reminder |

**4. Rolled to next month**

Nothing rolled. All 11 drafts placed.

**5. Rules check**

Full 30-day period: covered. No two gated posts are consecutive. Subjects and opening shapes change across the month. Time of day is not set because Dana supplied no result history by time.
