| Solid | 12 to 59 | 11 |

**4. Posting rhythm**

Your dates show the burst pattern: 11 posts between May 4 and May 20, then 3 weeks of silence, then bursts again in July and August. Six of your 7 Dead posts sat inside a burst week. Day-of-week ranking skipped: no single day has 5 or more posts behind it, and ranking on less produces confident nonsense.

**5. Caveats**

24 posts is a solid base. Two posts had no impression counts; they were tiered on comments only.

**The read:** both Hits open with a thing you did or a belief you corrected, and both went out in calm weeks. Six of the 7 Dead posts are announcements or templates published mid-burst. Run pattern-audit on this output to see the pattern with the evidence attached.
