# The $100K Ghostwriter

Eleven Claude skills that turn your real experience into LinkedIn posts in your voice, then help you plan and review a month of content. You supply the true events, opinions, and numbers. Missing facts stay marked until you confirm them.

The $100K Ghostwriter is the name of the collection, not an earnings or savings promise. These skills do not guarantee revenue, followers, leads, engagement, or any other result.

## Start with three skills

Install voice-match, post-writer, and voice-firewall, then open START-HERE.md.

1. voice-match studies writing you paste in and builds a short voice profile.
2. post-writer turns one true moment, result, lesson, or opinion into a draft with any missing facts marked.
3. voice-firewall rewrites lines that do not match your voice and marks factual statements you need to confirm.

The result is one finished draft in your voice. Every factual statement comes from what you supplied, and missing details are marked for you to confirm.

## The eleven skills

| Skill | What you end with |
|---|---|
| voice-match | A voice profile under 25 lines, built from your own writing. |
| post-writer | One draft plus three alternate openings, built only from facts you supplied and ready after you confirm any marked facts. |
| post-brief | The goal, opening, second line, proof, length, and weak claims decided before drafting. |
| voice-firewall | Every machine-sounding line rewritten in your voice, with factual claims marked for review. |
| hook-writer | 10 different opening lines, ranked, with the strongest one named. |
| angle-finder | 30 post angles from your real wins, numbers, opinions, stories, and old posts. |
| scoreboard | Your median result, every post grouped against it, and the failures listed first. |
| pattern-audit | The opening patterns to retire and the one pattern to use more. |
| visual-brief | The proof, layout, image text, and crop notes for one supporting visual. |
| calendar | A batch of ready posts dated across the period you choose, with a reason for every date. |
| tracker | One plain table showing whether each post is drafted, in review, ready, or dated. |

Install only what you need. Most skills work alone. Tracker records an editing check and a date from calendar before it marks a post as ready or dated.

## A month with the collection

Angle Finder turns your real material into angles. Post Brief locks the important decisions. Post Writer drafts. Voice Firewall cleans the language and marks facts for review. Visual Brief specifies an image when the post needs one. Calendar assigns dates, and Tracker keeps the current status visible. At month end, Scoreboard reads your results and Pattern Audit shows what to stop and what to repeat.
