# Start here

Your first result is one checked LinkedIn draft in your voice, ready for your final decision. This walkthrough uses three skills: voice-match, post-writer, and voice-firewall.

## Before you start

Have these ready:

- The three starter skills uploaded to Claude. INSTALL.md shows the steps.
- 3 to 10 LinkedIn posts you wrote, or a few work emails or notes. If you have none, voice-match can interview you.
- One true event, result, lesson, or opinion from your business.

## Step 1: teach Claude your voice

Open a new chat and send:

> Use voice-match.
> My name is: [your name]
> I do: [your role or business]
> I write for: [your audience]
> Here are 3 to 10 posts I wrote:
> [paste the full posts]

If you have no writing samples, send:

> Use voice-match and interview me.

You get a voice profile under 25 lines, including three exact lines from your writing. Read it. If a line feels wrong, say what is wrong and ask Claude to revise the profile. Save the final block in your notes.

## Step 2: choose one goal

Pick one:

- Gain relevant followers
- Build trust with buyers
- Collect leads
- Start sales conversations

One post can support more than one business result, but one main goal keeps the draft clear.

## Step 3: write the post

Send:

> Use post-writer.
> My goal: [your goal]
> My voice profile: [paste it]
> The idea: [1 to 3 true sentences]

A specific event gives the writer more to work with than a broad topic. Instead of “something about cash flow,” describe the moment: “A client thought March was profitable. The books showed why the cash was gone.”

Claude returns one draft, three alternate openings, and a recommendation. Any real number or detail you did not supply stays marked like this: [YOUR NUMBER: what belongs here].

## Step 4: confirm the facts

Fill every marked blank with a true detail. If you cannot confirm it, remove the sentence. Then read every factual statement against your own records. These skills cannot verify private business facts.

## Step 5: check the language

Send:

> Use voice-firewall on this post. My voice profile is above. Rewrite every flagged line.

Review each rewrite. If the verdict is FAIL, fix the named problems and run voice-firewall again.

## Step 6: make the final decision

Read the post once out loud. Make any final edit and decide whether to publish it. If your edit changes the meaning or adds a factual statement, run the edited version through voice-firewall once more.

## Done looks like this

You have one checked draft in your voice. You confirmed its factual statements, accepted or rejected the rewrites, and made the final publishing decision yourself.

## If something feels off

- The voice profile could describe anyone: paste more writing and rerun voice-match.
- The post feels generic: rerun post-writer with a specific event, example, or defensible opinion. Use a number when it is relevant; do not force one into an opinion post.
- The draft still sounds machine-written: tell voice-firewall which line bothers you and why, then ask it for three replacements.

## After your first post

Install another skill only when you need its job. Angle Finder finds material for a month. Hook Writer gives one idea 10 openings. Calendar dates ready posts. Scoreboard reads your real results at month end. README.md lists all eleven.
